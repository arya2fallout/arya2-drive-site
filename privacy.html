Privacy Policy

Arya 2 Drive Bridge · Last updated November 1, 2026

Purpose

Arya 2 Drive Bridge is a personal, locally hosted integration used by its operator to exchange commands and results through Google Drive using rclone. It is not a publicly available service.

Information accessed and used

When authorized, the application can access Google Drive files according to the permissions granted through Google OAuth. The bridge uses designated Google Drive folders for command input and result output, and may read, upload, or delete files as needed to run the operator's configured workflow.

Storage and retention

Commands and results can be stored in the operator's Google Drive account. The bridge also uses local temporary files and operational logs on the operator's computer. The operator controls access to the account, retention of result files, and deletion of stored information.

Sharing and selling

The application is not designed to sell Google user data or share it for advertising. Information may be transmitted to Google Drive, rclone, and other services expressly configured by the operator to perform requested tasks.

Security

Google OAuth authorizes access; the operator is responsible for protecting the local computer, OAuth credentials, and access to the Google Drive folders. This public website does not collect login credentials or OAuth tokens.

Control and revocation

The operator can remove files from Google Drive, stop the local bridge, and revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions about this personal integration, contact arya2.fallout@gmail.com.
