Arya 2 Drive Bridge

Arya 2 Drive Bridge is a private, personal automation project that uses Google Drive and rclone to exchange command and result files between an authorized Google Drive account and a locally hosted assistant.

The integration is for use by its operator, not offered as a public service. Access to Google Drive is subject to explicit Google account authorization.

Privacy policy

This site contains project information only. It does not host the bridge or its credentials.
