Arya 2 Drive Bridge · Last updated November 1, 2026

Purpose and scope

Arya 2 Drive Bridge is a personal, locally hosted automation integration that uses rclone and Google Drive to exchange command and result files for an operator-managed AI assistant. It is not offered as a public service.

Authorized use

Only the operator and people explicitly authorized by the operator may use the integration. Use requires authorization to access the relevant Google account and files and must comply with applicable Google service terms and policies.

How the integration works

The operator controls the local software, Google Drive credentials, folder permissions, and commands. Depending on the configured permissions, the integration may read, upload, edit, or delete files in the operator-authorized Drive account. The operator is responsible for reviewing commands and maintaining backups of important data.

Availability and changes

This personal project is provided as configured by its operator, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or suitability for any particular purpose. The operator may change, suspend, or discontinue the integration at any time.

Data and privacy

For details about how the project handles information, read the Privacy Policy. The public informational website does not request account passwords or OAuth tokens.

Revoking access

The operator can stop the local integration and revoke OAuth access through Google Account security settings.
